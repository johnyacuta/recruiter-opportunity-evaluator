# Evaluation criteria

## Hard gates
Evaluate remote/location eligibility, full-time employment, and compensation against the user's settings.

- **PASS** — explicit evidence supports the requirement.
- **FAIL** — explicit evidence shows a hard requirement is not met.
- **UNKNOWN** — information is missing, ambiguous, inaccessible, or insufficient.

Do not infer missing terms from job titles, company norms, or recruiter optimism. Without a configured salary floor, salary is UNKNOWN until clarified.

## Salary
Compare guaranteed base salary when the user configured a base-salary floor. Do not count bonus, equity, or commission unless configured. Entire range at/above floor: PASS; entirely below: FAIL; range straddles floor: UNKNOWN. Omitted/negotiable salary or unclear currency, period, or basis: UNKNOWN.

## Location and employment
Remote roles may restrict hiring by region. Compare restrictions with allowed work regions; unclear eligibility is UNKNOWN. Hybrid/on-site fails a fully remote requirement. Contract or part-time fails a full-time hard gate.

## Quality signals
Financial health and culture are signals by default, not automatic disqualifiers. A private company without public financial statements is UNKNOWN, not concerning. Explain evidence quality and conflicts.
