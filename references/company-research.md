# Company research guidance

Research only after every configured hard gate has PASS status, and only when supported by available search/browser tools. If a gate FAILS, stop and recommend Decline. If any gate is UNKNOWN, stop and request the missing information. In either case, do not search for the posting or research the company. The skill does not guarantee live research. If tools are unavailable after gates pass, say so and mark company signals UNKNOWN.

## Official job posting and compensation

After all hard gates pass, search the company's official careers site for the exact or clearly matching role. Compare title, location, and responsibilities so a similarly named but different opening is not mistaken for the recruiter opportunity. Prefer a direct official job posting link. Record the posting URL and date/access date where possible.

Check the matching posting for a published compensation range, salary basis, currency, location caveats, and employment details. Label it as company-posted compensation and keep it distinct from recruiter-provided compensation. Compare it with the user's configured floor according to the evaluation criteria. If the posting is unavailable, not an exact enough match, omits pay, or gives ambiguous compensation, mark the posting-based salary information UNKNOWN. Do not estimate from unrelated job listings, salary aggregators, or market averages. Do not bypass access controls or scrape sites contrary to their terms.

If the recruiter-stated compensation already passed the configured salary gate but the official posting's range conflicts with it or falls below the user's floor, flag the discrepancy and change the assessment to UNKNOWN or FAIL as supported by the evidence; do not conceal the conflict.

## Financial outlook
Prefer primary sources: regulatory filings, investor relations, official statements, and company announcements. Use reputable business reporting for context and label it as reporting. Consider cash/runway, debt, profitability, funding, layoffs, restructuring, or acquisition without treating one event as definitive. Include dates and distinguish current information from older reports.

A private company's lack of public financial data is not evidence of distress. Mark UNKNOWN unless credible evidence supports a qualified assessment.

## Workplace culture and Glassdoor
Use accessible review pages or summaries and other credible sources. Record Glassdoor rating, review count, and date when available. A score is limited and time-sensitive; consider sample size, recency, role/location relevance, and conflicting feedback.

Do not scrape Glassdoor or bypass login, paywalls, CAPTCHAs, rate limits, or access controls. Do not use unofficial APIs or reproduce reviews in bulk. If Glassdoor is unavailable, mark UNKNOWN and use other accessible sources as complementary context.

## Source hygiene
- Open sources where possible; a search snippet alone is insufficient for material claims.
- Link every material research claim directly to its source.
- Never invent citations, ratings, figures, or quotes.
- State when information is unavailable, stale, or conflicting.
- Label inference as an assessment, not a confirmed fact.
